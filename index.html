Cold Chain

The difference between
good and gone.

Africa holds 60% of the world's uncultivated arable land, yet 30% of harvests are lost to inadequate cold chain. That is not a statistic — it is food that never reaches a market, income that never reaches a farmer, and medicine that never reaches a patient. AFLO Cold Chain delivers -25°C to 8°C with ±0.3°C precision. Dual compressors. Independent temperature zones. A 6-hour power-loss buffer. Real-time cloud monitoring on every degree. Because a vaccine and an ice cream both deserve to be treated with absolute seriousness.

Cargo

One person.
An entire fleet.

Urban freight in Africa faces three obstacles: cost, congestion, and a chronic shortage of drivers. Driver wages alone consume over 40% of logistics budgets — before accounting for insurance, management overhead, and accident risk. Cargo was built specifically to remove those obstacles. 800 kg payload. 120 km range. L4 autonomous driving. A single fleet manager at a dashboard can coordinate 50 units simultaneously. We have logged 2 million kilometers on open urban roads across the continent. Zero accidents. Every line of code was written with intention.

Mobile Shop

Open a store.
Not a lease.

Opening a physical store in a rural community means rent, renovation, inventory, staffing — the costs accumulate before a single customer walks through the door. We asked ourselves: what if opening a store was as simple as parking a vehicle? Mobile Shop is the answer. 6 m² of retail space. Three-sided counter deployment. 30-minute setup from arrival to open. Pre-installed coffee machine, freezer, and point-of-sale system. Park at the office complex in the morning for breakfast. Move to the park at midday for coffee. Head to the night market for street food. Three locations. Zero leases. One vehicle. Commerce should be mobile.
